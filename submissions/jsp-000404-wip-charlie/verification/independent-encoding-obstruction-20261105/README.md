# Fixed-schedule encoding obstruction and retiming repair

This exact research certificate follows checkpoint
`727fc9262e161dcf16e888d5bc40785e64eb765e`. It refutes the fixed-schedule
independent-set representation proposed there. The original family-B bound
and a representation theorem selecting a prior retiming remain open.

## Checked obstruction

The input has nine wires and doubled half-period seven. Its 36 pair events
admit the stored adjacent-swap sequence from the initial order to its reverse.
All 84 triples satisfy betweenness, opposite-sign separation at least two,
and same-sign span at most five.

For any graph whose maximal independent sets preserve the signed rows, two
coordinates occurring positively in one row cannot form an edge. The checker
enumerates every subset of the remaining possible edges, under all 128
independent choices of coordinate polarity. This covers 22,752 graph/polarity
choices. Of these, 21,328 are triangle-free. For each triangle-free choice,
the checker enumerates every maximal independent set and verifies that at
least one row has no compatible completion.

Thus every triangle-free graph on the seven coordinates fails, even with
arbitrary coordinate-polarity choices. The common-polarity cases alone have
512 and 256 possible edge-subsets, of which 448 and 256 are triangle-free.

## Checked repair

The same wire order admits another feasible half-grid potential at period
7/2. The checker verifies all 72 consecutive or closing wire inequalities
for this potential. Its signed rows have nine distinct maximal-independent-
set completions in a five-cycle on `(0,3,4,5,6)` and an edge on `(1,2)`.
The graph has ten maximal independent sets in total.

This repaired potential was generated by shortest-path relaxation from
all-zero initial upper bounds. The finite certificate verifies its
feasibility and codes. A universal theorem about that canonical selection
rule remains unproved.
